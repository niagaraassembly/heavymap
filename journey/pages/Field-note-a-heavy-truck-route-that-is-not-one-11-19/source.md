Field note: a heavy truck route that is not one
expansion, field-note, hamilton, freight, truck-routes, roads

> A layer's title said one thing; its contents said another.

## What we saw
A City map titles one road layer "Heavy Truck Traffic Route". It holds all-vehicle daily volumes for all roads, counted between 2004 and 2020. Its older truck-route coding also disagrees with the current network: 839.6 kilometres in one against 597.9 kilometres of full-time routes in the other.

## What we do
We call it what it is: road segments with traffic volumes. Truck routes come from the current truck route network. Volumes are never presented as truck volumes.
