Preparation Bundles: freezing the question before the work
systems, assembly, make, questions, bundles

> Status: built and used locally since 2 November. Before any research is sent out, the questions are frozen into a bundle, and the bundle is reviewed on its own.

## The problem
Research drifts. A question gets reworded on the way, a reference is swapped, and by the time results come back nobody can say exactly what was asked.

## The bundle
A Preparation Bundle starts from a request in plain language, kept exactly as written. It then gathers a set of questions, each as an exact snapshot with its own framing, plus purpose, intended outcomes and references. The whole composition is bound by a hash. Later steps check that hash, so they can prove they are working from the same bundle.

## Two separate reviews
- **Question review** accepts, reworks or rejects the exact wording of each question.
- **Bundle review** accepts or rejects the exact composition.

Accepting the questions does not accept the bundle, and accepting the bundle does not accept the questions. A bundle is ready only when both hold.

## Reuse without guessing
A helper suggests existing questions that match a request. It works by matching place names and question metadata, not by interpreting the request. New questions proposed during composition are recorded with where they came from.

## Comments that stay attached
Comments can point at the whole bundle, its purpose, a single question or a single reference. If the target is removed, the comment stays visible and marked as unplaced.

## Where it leads
Question banks will belong to organisations and products, such as HeavyMap's bank of map questions. MAKE reads from those banks to build bundles and writes results back.
