Field note: 91,625 addresses on 8,603 spots
expansion, field-note, hamilton, addresses, units

> Apartment buildings stack many addresses on one point.

## What we saw
91,625 of Hamilton's address points share 8,603 locations: every unit in a building placed on the same spot. 356 points fall in no parcel at all, typically about seven metres away.

## What we do
Units are grouped under their civic address. Points that miss a parcel are matched to the nearest one within ten metres, and the rest are flagged with a stable tag so they can be listed.
