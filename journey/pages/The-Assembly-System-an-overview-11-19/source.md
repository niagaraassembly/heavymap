The Assembly System, an overview
systems, assembly, make, dispatch, join

> Status: built and partly in use. The Assembly System is the register behind all of our research: one front door for anything new, and a human decision at every step that matters.

## A register of profiles
The Assembly System holds a profile of every code base, product, dataset, goal and question we work with. A profile is a pointer, never the data itself. Only the Assembly System mints identifiers. Anything new comes in through one front door, and every change is made by setting, retiring or superseding a profile, with its history kept. Every command that changes something shows a preview first and needs a second, explicit step to apply.

## MAKE
MAKE is our research and processing service. It owns geography, through a register of regions and places, and it runs regional news research, Dispatch and Join.

## How research travels
1. **Question.** One thing we want to know.
2. **Preparation Bundle.** A frozen set of questions with framing and references, bound by a hash so later steps can prove nothing changed.
3. **Dispatch.** The approved instruction sent to whoever does the work. Nothing is fetched before a Dispatch is approved. One Dispatch gets one return.
4. **Digest.** The record of what came back, with counts and numbered limitations. Accepted or rejected as a batch.
5. **Packet.** Records decided one by one: accept, edit, reject, defer, flag, hold, exclude or needs more.
6. **Join.** An exact proposal of changes to the store, reviewed on its own.
7. **Bay.** An isolated staged copy built from an accepted Join.
8. **Promote.** The owner checks the plan's hash, then the change is applied.
9. **Edition and Publication.** Drafts. Publishing is always a human push.

## Gaps never close themselves
Unresolved items sit in a gap register with a type, priority, cost and a rule for closing them. Nothing closes without that rule being met, and blocked sources are never worked around.

## Nothing is answered until confirmed
Work can be handed out to machines and read back in, but no question counts as answered until a person confirms it.

## What is not built yet
Join handles reviewed research records today. It is not yet the processor for map data: the parcel spine and overlay joins in this Journey run in the Industrial Atlas's own code, outside Join. Bringing those spatial joins into Join, with the same exact change-sets and reviews, is planned work. Links to the scheduler and to more providers are pending.
