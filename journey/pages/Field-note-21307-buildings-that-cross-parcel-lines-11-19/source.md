Field note: 21,307 buildings that cross parcel lines
expansion, field-note, hamilton, buildings, parcels

> Buildings do not respect the lines on a parcel map.

## What we saw
21,307 building footprints overlap two or more parcels by more than a square metre. Some are real, such as buildings over lot lines. Others come from small mismatches between two maps drawn by different hands.

## What we do
Each building is assigned to the parcel it overlaps most, and every other overlap is kept in a table. Nothing is clipped away to look tidy.
