Timeline: from a UK atlas to the Hamilton map
origins, timeline

> Five months, three attempts and one city on the map. The dates below come from repositories, logs and data releases.

## June 2026
- **10 June.** The first Industrial Atlas starts life as a browser map of workshops and industrial land across the United Kingdom, built on OpenStreetMap.

## August 2026
- **18 August.** Work turns to Hamilton and the Niagara Peninsula. Every candidate source is checked against its live endpoint and written down with counts, fields and licences.
- **22 August.** A reconnaissance of the twelve Niagara municipalities finds one shared catalogue of 534 datasets instead of twelve portals.
- **23 August.** Hamilton's open data catalogue is swept: 463 datasets.
- **27 August.** A short spike tests the approach on Waterloo Region and Rochester, New York.
- **28 August.** The first Hamilton–Niagara atlas is paused at a clean boundary, with a unit index of 445,075 parcels, buildings and addresses built and nothing yet drawn.

## September 2026
- **20 September.** HeavyMap begins as its own project.
- **23 September.** The vocabulary for describing what we know about a parcel is written down as rules a machine can check. Counts for Brantford, Brant County and Norfolk County are verified by hand.

## October 2026
- **16 October.** A coverage study lists 606 dataset rows that touch Hamilton.
- **17 October.** The big day: the Hamilton catalogue is written, about a million features are pulled, every parcel gets a stable identifier, eleven overlay layers are joined to every parcel, and the first local map tiles are built.
- **18 October.** A beta gate for Hamilton is agreed: the checks a city must pass before it opens.

## November 2026
- **10 November.** The first static map of Hamilton's lower city and harbourfront.
- **16 November.** A self-hosted basemap in light and dark, and the first versioned Hamilton data release.
- **17 November.** Building permits go on the map, freight and infrastructure sources are profiled, and building footprints are ranked within their parcels.
- **18 November.** The public data discovery method is written down, and this Journey begins.
