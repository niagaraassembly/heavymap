Hamilton: building permits on the parcel map
expansion, hamilton, permits, development, run-1

> Twenty-one months of Hamilton's building permit reports, read record by record and tied to parcels. Development activity is now a layer you can see.

## The source
The City publishes a monthly digest of issued building permits. We read every month from January 2025 to September 2026: **8,986 permit records**.

## Tying permits to parcels
**8,812 of the 8,986 records** were linked to a parcel, touching **5,909 distinct parcels**. 1,905 records are in the focus set: industrial, commercial, logistics and multi-unit residential work, which the map emphasises.

## What the map shows
- At city scale, a mosaic of permit activity, with stronger cells where focus permits cluster.
- From zoom 14, the parcels themselves are highlighted.
- A period control to choose the months.
- On each parcel, its permit records, with the source's own wording.

## What it does not claim
A permit in a monthly report means it was issued that month. We do not infer whether the work started, finished or is still open. Owner and builder contact addresses are withheld.

## Three releases in a day
The development data went out as three small releases on 17 November. Each is a versioned, hash-checked set of files that never changes once published, so the map can always point at exactly what it showed.
